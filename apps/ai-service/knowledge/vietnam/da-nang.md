# Đà Nẵng

Đà Nẵng is a Vietnam beach and food destination with easy access to Hội An, Sơn Trà, Mỹ Khê beach, and mountain viewpoints.

Best season: February to August.
Food: Mì Quảng, bánh tráng cuốn thịt heo, seafood.
Culture guard: dress modestly in pagodas, avoid unsafe unlicensed rides late at night.
